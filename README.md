# My Favorite Movies: *The Greatest Showman*

**Genre:** Musical / Drama  
**Director:** Michael Gracey  
**Released:** 2017  
**Starring:** Hugh Jackman, Zac Efron, Zendaya, Rebecca Ferguson, Michelle Williams  

## Story Overview
*The Greatest Showman* celebrates the birth of show business and tells the story of **P.T. Barnum**, a visionary who rose from nothing to create a spectacle that became a worldwide sensation.  It’s a vibrant musical that combines catchy songs, heartwarming messages, and dazzling visuals.

## Why I Love It
- Inspiring story about chasing dreams  
- Energetic and powerful musical performances  
- Incredible choreography and cinematography  
- Songs like *“This Is Me”* and *“The Greatest Show”* always lift my mood
